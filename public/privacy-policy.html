SplitUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SplitUp collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SplitUp mobile application.

SplitUp is an expense-sharing application that allows users to create groups, record shared expenses, split expenses among group members, scan receipts, track balances, record settlements, and manage shared expense history.

SplitUp is operated by Usha Hasthi.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SplitUp may collect or process the following information.

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one

This information is used to create and manage your SplitUp account and identify you to members of expense-sharing groups.

Group and expense information

SplitUp may store information that you or other group members provide, including:

  • Group names and descriptions
  • Group membership
  • Expense titles and categories
  • Expense amounts
  • Participants in an expense
  • Information about who paid an expense
  • Individual expense shares
  • Itemized expense information
  • Recurring expense information
  • Settlement records
  • Dates and times associated with expenses and settlements

Receipts

If you choose to use receipt features, SplitUp may access a photo you capture or select from your device.

Receipt images may be processed to recognize text and identify information such as merchant names, purchased items, subtotal, tax, tip, discounts, and total amounts.

If a receipt is attached to an expense, the receipt image may be uploaded and stored using Firebase services so that it can be displayed with the associated expense.

Payment proof

If you choose to upload proof of a settlement or payment, SplitUp may store the image or file you provide together with the relevant settlement information.

You should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information in payment-proof images.

Notification information

SplitUp may use a device notification token to send notifications related to the app, such as group activity, expenses, settlements, or other account activity.

Notification tokens may be processed using Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Technical information

Firebase and related Google services used by SplitUp may process technical information necessary to provide their services, including device identifiers, network information, authentication information, and service-related diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

SplitUp uses information collected through the app to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display user names and profiles within groups
  • Create and manage expense-sharing groups
  • Record, calculate, and display shared expenses
  • Calculate balances between group members
  • Record settlements between users
  • Process and display receipt information
  • Store receipt and payment-proof attachments
  • Generate expense history, analytics, and reports
  • Send app-related notifications
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service
  • Provide and improve SplitUp functionality

3. Sharing of Information

Information associated with shared groups and expenses is visible to the appropriate members of those groups.

For example, group members may be able to see:

  • Your displayed name
  • Expenses involving the group
  • Who paid an expense
  • Expense participants and shares
  • Balances
  • Settlement information
  • Shared receipt or payment-proof information where applicable

SplitUp also relies on service providers to operate certain application functions.

4. Firebase and Google Services

SplitUp uses Google Firebase services that may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Cloud Storage for Firebase
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Hosting

These services provide authentication, database storage, file storage, notifications, and web hosting functionality.

Information processed through Firebase may be processed on Google's infrastructure in accordance with Google's and Firebase's applicable privacy and security practices.

5. Receipt Text Recognition

SplitUp may use Google ML Kit or similar device capabilities to recognize text contained in receipt images.

Recognized receipt information is used to help populate expense details. Users should review scanned information before saving an expense because automated text recognition may occasionally produce inaccurate results.

6. External Payment Applications

SplitUp may allow you to open third-party payment applications or services when settling an expense.

SplitUp does not process the actual transfer of money between users and does not require you to provide your payment-service password or payment-card credentials to SplitUp.

Payments completed through third-party applications are governed by the privacy policies and terms of those services.

7. Data Security

SplitUp uses Firebase and Google infrastructure and application security controls intended to protect user information.

Access to SplitUp data is restricted using authentication and database security rules designed to limit access to authorized users and appropriate group members.

Data transmitted between the SplitUp application and Firebase services is transmitted using secure network connections provided by those services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention

SplitUp retains account and application data for as long as necessary to provide the application's services or until you delete your account, subject to the shared-data considerations described below.

Some group expenses, settlements, or similar shared records may involve multiple users. Certain shared historical records may remain available to other group members after an account is deleted where retaining the record is necessary to preserve their expense history and balances.

When this occurs, personal profile information associated with the deleted account is removed or anonymized, and the former user may be displayed as "Deleted user".

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your SplitUp account directly from the application.

To delete your account:
  1. Open SplitUp.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

When your account is deleted, SplitUp deletes or removes personal account information including your authentication account, personal profile details, applicable profile photos, personal notification data, and notification tokens.

Shared expense records may remain as described in the Data Retention section above, with identifying profile information removed or anonymized.

You may also review account-deletion instructions at:

SplitUp Account Deletion

Account deletion is permanent.

You may create a new SplitUp account later, including with the same email address. A newly created account is treated as a new account and will not automatically restore or reconnect to data associated with a previously deleted account.

10. Your Choices

Depending on your device and the features you use, you may:

  • Edit your SplitUp profile information
  • Choose whether to upload a profile photo
  • Choose whether to scan or upload receipts
  • Choose whether to upload payment proof
  • Manage app notification permissions through your device
  • Delete your SplitUp account

11. Children's Privacy

SplitUp is intended as an expense-management and expense-sharing application and is not designed specifically for children under 13.

If you believe that personal information from a child has been provided to SplitUp improperly, please contact us using the information below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When the policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.
