Delete Your SplitUp Account

SplitUp allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal profile information.

Delete your account from the SplitUp app

  1. Open the SplitUp app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Review the confirmation message.
  6. Select Delete account again to confirm.

After confirmation, your SplitUp authentication account will be permanently deleted.

What data is deleted?

When your account is deleted, SplitUp removes or deletes personal information associated specifically with your account, including:

  • Your SplitUp authentication account
  • Your name and email address from your active profile
  • Your profile photo, when stored by SplitUp
  • Your personal notification data
  • Your device notification tokens

Shared expense history

Some expense, group, or settlement records may involve other SplitUp users. These shared records may need to remain available to those users so that their expense history and balances remain accurate.

When your account is deleted, your personal profile information is removed and your identity may appear as "Deleted user" in shared historical records.

You may create a new SplitUp account in the future, including with the same email address. A newly created account is treated as a new account and will not restore or reconnect to data associated with your previously deleted account.

Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Can't access the app?

If you are unable to access the SplitUp app and would like to request deletion of your account, contact the SplitUp developer using the support email listed on the SplitUp Google Play Store listing.
